## 7. Smoke **[W]**

SM plans (design `20261027-sm-plans.md` §8):
1. single replica: `/target` +1/-1 and a one-item plan;
2. multi replica: `at_least awake+2` -> both `/wake_up` in one hold (`phases_ms`);
3. multi-model: a plan sleeping 2-3 models + waking 2 on free GPUs -> hold about one op, not the sum;
4. donor -> receiver: named sleep + receiver on the freed GPU (wave 2), relay `donors`; gpu-truth never shows two awake;
5. partial failure: `fail_wake` hook -> compensating sleep;
6. TP2: 14b receiver over two 7b donors;
7. SM pod killed during wave 2 -> journals settle, `/v2/audit` clean (one manual call, no polling).

TSS signal sanity (a few minutes of `observe active` under load): Z finite while busy (no NaN / inf in
`trs_calc_result`), no `samples_incomplete` storm, no basis refusal in the controller log, O1 hold about 20 s.

Runner: one smoke campaign (~7 min slices, `max_attempts: 1`, spec copied from `campaign-smoke3-v2-20261026.yaml`)
with: the TRE arm; one perturbed sensitivity arm (e.g. `sens-theta-m20`: registry applied, restored, restore
annotation cleared); the APA arm; one baseline arm (tokenscale); after step 3, one alt arm (e.g. `sig-queue`).
`first_run_check.py` on each arm.
